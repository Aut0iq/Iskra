# Iskra ✦

<img src="docs/media/iskra.gif" alt="Iskra, the raspberry flame" width="160">

The tiny raspberry flame that lives inside [Zeron](https://zeron.sh).

She shows agent status, keeps your usage cache warm, and — if you let her —
sets up camp on your Linux desktop. She watches the build so you don't have
to. Glaring at red CI is a personality trait, not a repair strategy — but it
does pair nicely with the `wall_right_sadness` animation set.

## What she does

- Reports what your agent is up to, without asking it awkward questions.
- Shows cached usage numbers — read-only, no telemetry, no drama.
- Optionally wanders your Wayland desktop as a small pixel-art pet.

She doesn't fix your CI. She *comments* on it. Emotionally.

## Install

**Zeron → Settings → Plugins → Install** →
[latest stable release](https://github.com/Energy-Quant/Iskra/releases/latest),
or select its signed `.zeron-plugin` file. Review the author and permissions —
Iskra reads status, she doesn't sign anything herself.

The plugin/avatar tools are portable; the desktop pet is Linux/Wayland only —
she knows where she's welcome. [Full installation guide](docs/INSTALLATION.md) ·
[Desktop & ashell](docs/ASHELL.md) · [Make your avatar](docs/AVATARS.md)

Building with us? [Contributing](CONTRIBUTING.md) · [Development & delivery](docs/DEVELOPMENT.md).
Nightlies are unsigned developer previews — fun, but not signed. Stable keys
stay home, like a responsible flame.
