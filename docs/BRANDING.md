# Current laptop branding

The owner selected **Raspberry Flame**, option 1 of the second design
preview, on 2026-11-14. The earlier amber Ember design and the other
preview options are not approved for production.

## Approved Mark

The mark is an original, compact 24 by 24 pixel flame: an asymmetric
raspberry silhouette with three upward tongues, a soft pink highlight,
a small mint inner flame and a warm-white accent.

| Role | Color |
| --- | --- |
| Main flame | `#CD3F78` |
| Highlight | `#F5A2BB` |
| Inner flame | `#62C6CD` |
| Accent | `#FFF0D8` |

The approved production masters are `assets/branding/raspberry-flame.png`
and `.svg`. They preserve the owner-selected 24 by 24 geometry.
The original mark falls under the project's artwork terms
(`ARTWORK.md`, `LICENSE-ARTWORK`), to the extent applicable rights are
held.

## Asset Editing Contract

Replace only the logo and its antialiased fringe inside the laptop
lid. Reconstruct the underlying lid locally, then project the approved
mark to its perspective. Preserve the laptop rim, fingers, hinge,
character, pose, lighting, timing, contact geometry and transparency.
Pixels outside the recorded repair masks must remain unchanged.

Do not globally replace cyan: it also occurs in clothing and tools.
Do not regenerate a whole character sheet to change a small badge.
Keep baseline copies and per-image repair masks as local verification
evidence. Keep new postprocess provenance separate from historical
generation reports.

## Prospective Generation Instructions

Use this text for future laptop branding, together with the approved
master as the visual reference:

> On each visible outward-facing laptop lid, show exactly one original
> Iskra Raspberry Flame badge, matching the approved 24 by 24 pixel
> master. Preserve its raspberry outer flame, pink highlight, mint
> inner flame and warm-white accent. Adapt only its scale and
> perspective to the lid. Do not draw Arch Linux, Nyarch, an A-shaped
> arch, a wordmark, or another third-party brand. The character remains
> in her established illustrated style; only the small badge is pixel
> artwork.

Earlier image-generation records are retained only in the private working
archive. Their old Arch instructions are superseded by this document.

## Scope And History

Replacing the current V6 production images does not by itself clean
older fallback artwork, rejected source sheets, old archives, or Git
history. Release documentation must identify the actual edited scope.
Do not delete or rewrite historical material without separate owner
authorization, and do not describe a selective current-asset change as
a legal clearance of the entire repository.
